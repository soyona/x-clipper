Editing notes:

- Use direct cuts or 250 ms crossfades; do not simulate clicks that were not recorded.
- Keep the browser UI screenshots pixel-accurate. Do not rebuild or animate individual controls.
- Use subtitles for the full voiceover. Keep each subtitle to two lines.
- Background music is optional and must remain below the voiceover.
- End card must remain visible for at least three seconds.
